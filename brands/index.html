The catalogue above is a deliberate subset.

Most of what VAN manufactures leaves the plant under somebody else’s brand. Those formulations are not published here and will not be — that is the agreement we make with the companies we make them for. What we can show you is what the lines are capable of.

Physical forms

Granular · powder · crystalline soluble · liquid · pellet · water-dispersible granule · coated prill. The same composition is made in more than one form rather than the form dictating the composition.

Pack range

From a 1 L bottle to a 1000 kg bulk bag and a 1000 L IBC. Trade packs, estate packs and contract-farming volumes are packed on the same line as the retail unit.

Registration, run by us

VAN runs the PSQCA registration under your brand rather than handing you a specification and leaving you to it. The licence route is already worked.

Quality control

A PNAC-accredited laboratory, ISO/IEC 17025:2017, Accreditation No. LAB 336. Every batch is tested. Batches outside the working window are rejected, not averaged into the ones that passed.
