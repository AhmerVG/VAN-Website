Crop Force

NPK 15-15-15  ·  NPK 12-12-18

Balanced NPK to PS 933, in two grades: an equal-thirds 15-15-15 for early growth, and a 12-12-18 weighted to potash for the stage where Pakistani programmes usually run short.

Application & dosage

Crop Force is a compound NPK blend for soil and foliar application. Doses below are drawn directly from VAN's crop nutrition plans (grade 15-15-15) and VAN agronomy guidance (grade 12-12-18); other crops are covered in the full nutrition plans.
