Application & dosage

VL-NPK is a foliar nutrient supplement. The general rate applies to all field crops and orchards; the featured crops below carry stage-specific programmes from the locked VAN nutrition plans (2025 set).

Foliar rate — all crops

1 L per acre in 200 L water. Apply at mid-life, again at the fruiting stage, and whenever a stress event calls for it — heat, cold, waterlogging, salinity, a chemical knock or a transplant check. The dilution is part of the rate: cutting the water doubles the concentration on the leaf.
