V-Potash Plus

10-0-40 + Mg · B · chelated Fe (300 ppm each)

Water-soluble potash for fertigation, carrying magnesium, boron and chelated iron at the levels declared on the label. They are co-factor levels for the potassium, not a nutrient dose in their own right — a crop short of magnesium or boron still needs the product that supplies it.

Application & dosage

VAN agronomy guidance (Jul 2026): 10 kg/acre for field crops; for iron-responsive horticulture, split the dose across peak potash-uptake stages. Iron-coated (chelated Fe-EDDHA) — prioritise crops and soils with iron demand.
