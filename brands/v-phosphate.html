V-Phosphate10-44-0 · coated with Fe, Mn & Cu

Water-soluble phosphorus for the mid-life stage, micronutrient-fortified for alkaline soils. Where granular P locks up as calcium phosphate, V-Phosphate delivers its 44% P₂O₅ through fertigation — with iron, manganese and copper riding on every particle.

Why it works in Pakistani soils

Bypasses fixation

Fully soluble P applied with irrigation reaches roots before soil calcium can bind it.

Stage-matched

Mid-life P demand — root expansion, tillering, early reproductive push — is exactly when this grade is applied.

Micros included

Fe, Mn and Cu coating counters the micronutrient lock-out typical of pH-8 soils.
