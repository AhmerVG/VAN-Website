Why it works in Pakistani soils

P at mid-life

By mid-life, much of the sowing phosphate has been fixed by soil calcium. A liquid dose through the water reaches the root directly, without waiting on the soil.

Liquid precision

Applied through fertigation, dosing is uniform plant-to-plant, unlike broadcast granules.

Already made for partners

The same formulation is sold under partner brands today.

Application & dosage

Doses as printed on the V-Germinator Pro label. Apply about 40 to 45 days after the first phosphate application, or at mid-life, through the irrigation water.
