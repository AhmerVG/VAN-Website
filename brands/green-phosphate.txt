Green Phosphate is the 6-32 grade of the
VAN NP Range
.
It is the established, registered member of a family of phosphate blends engineered against calcium fixation in calcareous soil — known release kinetics, coated phosphate, dual release and calcium interception. Two further grades extend it — NP 5-40 and NP 8-38 + sulfur — and neither carries a VAN brand: both are held in VAN’s formulation library for a partner to take under their own name.

Why it works in Pakistani soils
Acid microzone
Each granule creates a locally acidic environment that keeps phosphate in solution where roots can take it.
Humic carrier
The humic layer chelates calcium, slows fixation, and feeds soil biology around the granule.
Basal workhorse
Designed for the sowing window — where most of Pakistan's P is applied and most of it is normally lost.
