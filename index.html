What this soil does

Three ways the ground takes your fertilizer back.

Why Pakistan must shift ›

Ammonia to the air, phosphate to calcium, potash the crop never got — three losses, and a product built against each.

Nitrogen · loss 1 of 3

Up as ammonia, down as nitrate.

Above pH 8, surface urea leaves as ammonia before the crop can use it. What dissolves moves down with the irrigation water.

Vital UreaSulfur Coated Urea · N 32% min · S 13% minRead the evidence ›
Phosphate · loss 2 of 3

Calcium closes in and locks it.

Calcareous soil binds phosphate with free calcium and takes most of it back before the root arrives.

Green PhosphateP₂O₅ 32% · N 6%Read the evidence ›
Potash · loss 3 of 3

The root goes looking.

One kilogram of potash goes on for every eighty-three of nitrogen — and the crop fills from potash.
