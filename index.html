01The national picture

Pakistan is not short of fertilizer. It is short of nutrition that reaches the crop.

The whole case, with every chart and source →
63kg/acre

Nutrient Pakistan puts on an acre of cropland in a year. The world average is 47. FAO publishes these as 156 and 117 kg a hectare.

83: 1

Nitrogen against potash, by tonne. Crops are not fed on one nutrient.

−22%

What a kilogram of nutrient returns in wheat now, against the year 2000. Derived.

≈25%

The share of that nitrogen a crop actually takes up. In the United States it is about 72%.

4 figures, 4 published sources (Our World in Data / FAO, FAOSTAT, the Pakistan Economic Survey, Lassaletta et al. 2014), each charted and cited on the knowledge page. Derived figures are marked derived. The ground under them, 770,160 Punjab samples across 36 districts, is on the soil page.
