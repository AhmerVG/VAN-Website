01The national picture

Pakistan is not short of fertilizer. It is short of nutrition that reaches the crop.

The whole case, with every chart and source →
63kg/acre

Nutrient Pakistan puts on an acre of cropland in a year. The world average is 47. FAO publishes these as 156 and 117 kg a hectare.

83: 1

Under all 4 of those figures sits the ground itself, and Punjab has measured it 770,160 times across 36 districts. That survey is on this site in full, district by district and parameter by parameter, with what it does not measure marked as plainly as what it does.

Where a figure is VAN's own arithmetic on published inputs it is marked derived, and no performance figure is claimed for any product.
