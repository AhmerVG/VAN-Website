01
What is on site
Nine lines, one roof.
Line
What it does
Compound granulation
Granulation in which every granule carries the complete declared analysis, so nothing segregates between the bag and the field.
Bulk blending
Physical blending of single-nutrient and specialty granules to a target ratio, with compatibility checked before the batch rather than after it.
Sulfur coating
Commercial scale. The line behind VAN’s sulfur-coated urea, running since before the grade had a market in Pakistan.
Liquids
The full VL liquid range, from foliar concentrates to fertigation grades.
Organics and humates
Compost and organic-carbon carriers, humate in crystal, powder, pellet and liquid forms.
Biological laboratory
Established, with test production alongside it. Phosphorus-solubilizing bacteria today; a liquid inoculant and a mycorrhizal product in development, all on locally isolated strains.
Micronutrient coating
Boron and iron coated directly onto the granule. The house speciality. The micronutrient cannot segregate in the bag, and it lands wherever the granule lands. Biological coating is in development.
Filling and storage
Pack formats from one litre to bulk, with finished-goods storage on site.
Repeat lots
Orders above a single lot run as repeat batches to the same frozen specification.
02
Quality control
The method column is the one that matters in an audit.
Every production lot is released against a certificate of analysis from VAN’s own laboratory. The format is consistent across the catalogue: physical status, moisture, particle size and pH, followed by the chemical assays, each carrying its specification, its result and the method used to obtain it. That is why COA data, not marketing copy, is the source for every physical property published on this site.
Rule
What it means
No lot ships without a COA
Released against the frozen specification, every time.
Every assay names its method
So a third-party result can be compared like for like.
A specification change is documented
The partner is told before it takes effect. VAN does not substitute silently.

03
The research farm
Every formulation is tested on calcareous field soil, not only on the bench.
VAN’s first agricultural experimental station was established in 2009 and the company runs its own research farm, staffed by agricultural scientists, for product trials. The farm is where a failure gets found out before a client’s season depends on it.
Recognition for the R&D programme has come from the Pakistan Innovation Foundation and from the Market Development Facility of the Government of Australia.
Standards the plant works to: certificate numbers and issuing bodies are provided on request and in any partner audit pack. Plant visits are part of most partner conversations, and for a co-manufacturing decision they are usually the point at which it becomes a real one.
