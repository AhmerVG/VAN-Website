The soil, measured
· Punjab
770,160 samples. One condition.
Punjab tested three-quarters of a million soil samples for pH, organic matter, phosphorus, potash, calcium carbonate and five micronutrients. Read together, they describe one soil: alkaline, calcareous, almost without carbon, short of phosphorus, potash, zinc and boron in most of them, and never tested for sulfur.

What this ground costs the country
More product on this soil is not the answer. The soil takes it back first.
Punjab's ground is alkaline and calcareous.
96.1% of samples above pH 7.5
, mean carbonate 6.53%. On that soil, nutrient applied per hectare has risen by more than two thirds since 2000 while wheat yield rose by less than a third.
Roughly one kilogram of applied nitrogen in four reaches the crop.
What changes the outcome is
the form the nutrient arrives in and where it is put
, not the tonnage. That is what a value added fertilizer is, and it is why this soil demands one.

Method & caveats
Thirty-six district workbooks from the Punjab soil testing programme, each row one georeferenced sample carrying district, tehsil, markaz, union council and mauza identifiers alongside the laboratory determinations. Every row was read; no sub-sampling. Records without a valid pH were excluded; 770,160 remained. Province figures are weighted by district sample count.
Age
The sampling window is roughly 2016–2018. These figures describe a structural baseline, not present-day status.
Observational
Survey data with no controlled treatments. It establishes conditions with very high confidence. It cannot establish mechanism, for that, controlled and peer-reviewed work is the appropriate evidence.
Thresholds
Deficiency shares use Punjab’s working thresholds: organic matter below 0.86%, available phosphorus below 7 ppm (adequate at 15), available potash below 180 ppm, zinc below 1.0 ppm, boron below 0.5 ppm.
Repairs
The Sheikhupura workbook carries a duplicated header column; it was re-parsed with the offset corrected. Jhelum returns no valid zinc data. Physically impossible values were bounded before averaging.
Bounds and repairs, as recorded in the module.
