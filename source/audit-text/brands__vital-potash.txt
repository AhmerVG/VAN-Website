Flagship · a grower’s question
I fed the crop well and it still filled light. The bolls dropped, the grain stayed thin. Why?
1
The crop grows on nitrogen and fills on potash
Potash moves the sugar the leaf makes into the grain, the tuber or the fruit, and thickens the cell wall against heat, drought and disease. Pakistan applies about 1 kg of potash for every 83 kg of nitrogen.
2
Made for the fill window
N 11, K₂O 44, fully water soluble. It goes on at grand growth and maturity, through the fertigation or flood water, when the crop has stopped building leaf and started filling. The small nitrogen share keeps the plant working through fill without pushing new leaf.
3
Boron rides on the granule, in 2 forms
Boron carries sugar to the flower and sets the seed and the fruit. Since 2014 VAN has coated it onto the potash granule on its own line, developed and built locally, in 2 forms on 1 granule: sodium borate for fast action and calcium borate for slow release. It lands where the potash lands and cannot separate in the bag.
