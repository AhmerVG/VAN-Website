How it works
8 things you tell it. 5 things it tells you.
Your field, as it is
1
Crop and variety, from Pakistan’s list, not a generic one.
2
District and soil, from the Punjab survey’s 770,160 samples and VAN’s own; a laboratory report overrides it.
3
Target yield, in maunds per acre, against what the district can carry.
4
Sowing date, the day, not the month.
5
Irrigation, canal, tube well or both, and the schedule you can keep.
6
Plant population, seed rate and stand.
7
Cropping pattern, what the field carried before: rice–wheat, cotton–wheat, cane and ratoon.
8
Practices, weed window, top-dressing and harvest window.
Your yield, your score, your plan
1
Your attainable yield, with the discipline you described, on your field.
2
Your discipline score, which practices carry the target, and which one is costing you most.
3
The nutrients in kilograms. N, P₂O₅, K₂O, the secondaries and the micros, stage by stage, after the soil’s own contribution.
4
The VAN products that deliver them. Product, stage, method and pack, from VAN’s published crop programmes.
5
What each slip costs, and the way back.
What it is built on
The figures come from ground VAN owns and samples VAN read.
Every figure it prints carries its source: VAN’s own trials first, published Pakistani research with its citation second.
