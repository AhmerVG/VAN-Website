Soil Poverty Series · No. 1
Soil Poverty: Punjab’s Potash. As potash runs short, urea and DAP buy less yield.
Punjab’s crops take about 940,000 tonnes of potash out of the soil a year; fertilizer puts back 36,000. Most of 20,000 model runs put the Punjab average below 100 ppm between 2027 and 2044. A report by Tahir Abbas, with the full PDF.
