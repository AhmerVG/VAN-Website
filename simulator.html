How it works

8 things you tell it. 5 things it tells you.

Your field, as it is

  1. 1Crop and variety, from Pakistan’s list, not a generic one.
  2. 2District and soil, the Punjab survey’s 770,160 samples and VAN’s own set the soil prior; a laboratory report overrides it.
  3. 3Target yield, in maunds per acre, against what the district can carry.
  4. 4Sowing date, the day, not the month.
  5. 5Irrigation, canal, tube well or both, and the schedule you can keep.
  6. 6Plant population, seed rate and stand.
  7. 7Cropping pattern, what the field carried before: rice–wheat, cotton–wheat, cane and ratoon.
  8. 8Practices. Weed window, top-dressing, harvest window.

Your yield, your score, your plan

  1. 1Your attainable yield, with the discipline you described, on your field.
  2. 2Your discipline score, which practices carry the target, and which one is costing you most.
  3. 3The nutrients in kilograms. N, P₂O₅, K₂O, the secondaries and the micros, stage by stage, after the soil’s own contribution.
  4. 4The VAN products that deliver them. Product, stage, method and pack, from the crop programmes already on this site.
  5. 5What each slip costs, and the way back.
