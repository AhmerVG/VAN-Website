EST. 2010 · RESEARCH FARM SINCE 2009 · LAHORE, PAKISTAN

We were in a field before we were a company.

کمپنی بعد میں بنی، ہم کھیت میں پہلے سے تھے

We opened our first research farm in 2009, 1 year before the company was incorporated in 2010. Trials have run on our research farms in every season since 2009. By 2026, these trials have run for 17 years.

We design and revise our formulations with the trial results from our research farms and with the results of the 770,160 soil samples that the Punjab soil testing programme tested in about 2016 to 2018. The samples came from 36 districts of Punjab. 1 result of the programme is that 69.1% of the 770,160 samples had a pH above 8. The soil test results come from the 36 district workbooks of the Punjab soil testing programme. We name the programme as the source of these results.

We hold 34 licences from the Pakistan Standards and Quality Control Authority (PSQCA). Of these licences, 22 are for our own brands. The other 12 licences are for registered formulations that a partner company can sell under its own brand.

Chronology

2009 to 2026

These are the dated milestones of our company from 2009 to 2026.

  1. 2009

    The first research farm

    We opened our first research farm in 2009, 1 year before the company was incorporated.

  2. 2010

    The company and the land

    Vital Agri Nutrients (Pvt) Ltd was incorporated in 2010. We bought the land for the plant in the same year.

  3. 2011

    Building the plant

    Building work on the plant started in 2011. We made our first blended fertiliser in the same year.

  4. 2014

    The coating line

    We added a coating line to the plant in 2014. Its first product was boron-coated nitro potash. We now sell this product as Vital Potash.

  5. About 2016 to 2018

    The Punjab soil testing programme

    The Punjab soil testing programme tested 770,160 samples from 36 districts in about 2016 to 2018. The results come from the programme's 36 district workbooks. We use the results of the programme when we design the nutrient content of our products.

  6. 2017

    The Vital Agri Centers

    We opened our own shops, the Vital Agri Centers, in 2017.

  7. 2019

    Vital Urea

    We launched Vital Urea, our sulfur-coated urea, in 2019.

  8. 2023

    Potassium recovered from the ash of crop residue

    We made our first commercial batch of potassium recovered from the ash of crop residue in 2023. Recovery of silicon from the same ash is still in research and development.

  9. 2024

    Laboratory accreditation and CropLife membership

    The Pakistan National Accreditation Council (PNAC) accredited our laboratory to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 in 2024, under accreditation number LAB 336. The accreditation covers 8 of the laboratory's 21 fertiliser tests. We have been a full member of CropLife Pakistan since 2024.

  10. 2026

    Patent and biological laboratory

    Pakistan Patent 144684 was granted in 2026 for Vital Urea, our sulfur-coated urea. Pakistan Patent 144684 covers the coating of Vital Urea. We set up our first biological laboratory in 2026.

VAN Lab · PNAC · ISO/IEC 17025:2017

LAB 336 and blind testing

We test every batch before it leaves the plant. Our laboratory is accredited by PNAC to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 under accreditation number LAB 336. The accreditation covers 8 of the laboratory’s 21 fertiliser tests.
