Soil Poverty Series · No. 1

Soil Poverty: Punjab’s Potash. Where available soil potassium is low, crops can take up less of the nitrogen and phosphorus in urea and diammonium phosphate (DAP)

Punjab’s 5 main crops are wheat, rice, sugarcane, maize and cotton. In the 2023-24 crop year, these 5 crops removed an estimated 940,000 tonnes of potash (as K₂O) from the soil. In the same year, about 36,000 tonnes of potash was put back into the soil in fertiliser (offtake records of the National Fertilizer Development Centre).

A Vital Agri Nutrients report

The cost of low nitrogen use efficiency in Pakistan

Pakistan’s nitrogen use efficiency (NUE) was 29.1% in 2023, according to an estimate by the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO). The 29.1% means that Pakistan’s harvested crops contained about 29 kg of nitrogen for every 100 kg of nitrogen added to cropland in 2023. The 100 kg counts nitrogen from all sources, including fertiliser and manure.

Recovering potassium from crop residue ash
