Find the plan for your crop

Each plan gives the amount of each product to apply per acre at every crop stage. Each of our crop plans is written for alkaline, calcareous soil.

    The season dial

    When each crop plan can start

    Each ring of the dial shows 1 of our 28 crop plans. Its coloured arc covers the months in which the plan can start: at sowing, at planting or at the start of the season. A plan with 2 start windows has 2 arcs. The needle marks today's date. Choose a month to see its crop plans, or choose an arc to see 1 plan.

    All 28 crop plans

    Choose your crop

    Our 28 crop plans are sorted into 5 groups. The number beside each group is the count of crop plans in that group. Choose a crop to open its plan.

    For 24 of the 28 plans, the Nutrition creator changes the amount of each product per acre to suit your soil test. If you have no soil test, you can choose your Punjab district. The Nutrition creator then uses the median soil test results for that district from the Punjab soil testing programme. These are typical values for the district, not readings from your own field.

    The soil adjustment in the Nutrition creator is a pilot. Check any change with your VAN dealer before you apply it.

    Calcareous soil is soil that contains free lime. Soil with a pH above 7 is alkaline. In the Punjab soil testing programme, 69.1% of the 770,160 samples tested in about 2016 to 2018 had a pH above 8. These samples came from alkaline soil.
