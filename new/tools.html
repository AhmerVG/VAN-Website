    Read this before you act

    About the figures on this page

    The tools on this page use the data and rules of the tools on van.com.pk, with 3 differences.

    1. This page takes 1 to 500 acres. The Nutrition creator on van.com.pk takes 1 to 50 acres.
    2. This page takes 3 soil readings. The Nutrition creator on van.com.pk takes up to 10 readings. For a field with a full soil report, use the Nutrition creator on van.com.pk.
    3. The simulator on this page lists every estimated yield gain above 0.2 maunds per acre. The simulator on van.com.pk lists the 3 largest estimated yield gains.
    Soil figures
    The soil figures come from 770,160 samples of the Punjab soil testing programme. The samples were tested in about 2016 to 2018. The figures describe the soil as it was then.
    District figures
    A district figure describes the samples of the whole district. Your own field needs its own soil test.
    Map shapes
    Each district is drawn from the squares of the Soil Atlas grid that hold samples. Each square is about 5.5 km by 4.8 km. A gap in the map is ground with no sample.
    Crop plans
    Each timeline follows our published crop plan. A crop plan is a general plan, set per acre for a typical crop on a typical soil. It does not replace a soil test.
    Soil adjustment
    The soil adjustment is a pilot. It uses the rules of the Nutrition creator on van.com.pk.
    Wheat stage dates
    The wheat stage dates are calculated from growing degree days. Growing degree days measure the heat a crop receives. The calculation uses Lahore’s temperature normals for 1991 to 2020. The dates therefore describe a normal season at Lahore.
    Estimated yield
    The estimated yield in the simulator is a model estimate. The model is a pilot.
