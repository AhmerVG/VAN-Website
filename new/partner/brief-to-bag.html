How a partner product is developed and registered

A new product developed to your brief goes through 6 development stages, from feasibility to PSQCA registration, and we run all 6.

  1. 01The sequence
  2. 02What you receive
  3. 03Where registration sits
01The sequence

The 6 development stages, in order

What happens
FeasibilityStarting from your brief, we confirm the market, the crop, the crop stage, the target price, the pack format and the constraint the product must overcome. We also study the soil, crop and water of the territory the product is aimed at, so that the formulation suits those conditions rather than average conditions.
BenchBench work in our laboratory on ratio, form, coating, chelation, solubility, pH and compatibility.
PilotThe pilot batch is made on the same equipment that will make the commercial lot, so a formulation that works in the pilot also works at full scale.
TrialsTrials on our research farms and, if you want them, on your own demonstration plots.
DossierWe select the PSQCA standard and prepare the dossier.
RegistrationWe handle the PSQCA registration, and PSQCA issues 1 manufacturing licence for the product, valid across Pakistan.

After registration, we start commercial production and provide a certificate of analysis for each production batch, technical documentation and training material for your field team. Repeat batches are made to the frozen specification, and any change to that specification is documented and never made without telling you.

02What you receive

03Where registration sits

We run registration as part of product development

A product can miss its selling season even when the formulation is finished and the pilot has succeeded, because the licence takes time to obtain: we complete our 2 registration steps within 3 weeks, and the regulator’s 2 steps typically take a further 1 to 3 months. We therefore treat registration as part of development, not as a task left to you afterwards. The team that took our own products through PSQCA registration handles yours. We hold 35 PSQCA licences. Of these licences, 23 are for our own brands. The other 12 licences are for registered formulations that a partner company can sell under its own brand.

The licence count is as of November 2026. The other figures are as of July 2026.
