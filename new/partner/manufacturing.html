Manufacturing & quality

VAN’s plant runs 5 production lines: granular, coated, liquid, water-soluble and compounded. Quality control is done in the analytical laboratory on the same site, and our research farms test every formulation in the field.

01What is on site

5 production lines, 2 laboratories

LineWhat it does
GranularPhysical blending of single-nutrient and speciality granules to a target ratio, with compatibility checked before the batch rather than after it.
CoatedSulfur coating at commercial scale; this is the line that makes Vital Urea. It also coats boron and iron directly onto the granule, so the micronutrient cannot separate in the bag and is applied wherever the granule is applied. The coated line was added in 2014.
LiquidOur full liquid range (the VL- products), from foliar concentrates to fertigation grades.
Water-solubleFully soluble grades for fertigation and foliar feeding.
CompoundedCompound granulation, in which every granule carries the complete declared analysis, so nothing separates between the bag and the field.

Orders larger than a single lot are made as repeat lots to the same frozen specification. Pack formats range from 0.5 L to bulk, and finished goods are stored on site.

LaboratoryWhat it does
Analytical laboratoryAccredited by PNAC (Pakistan National Accreditation Council) to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (LAB 336) for 8 of its 21 fertiliser tests. Every batch is tested here, and a lot ships only when all its batches have passed.
Biological laboratoryOpened in 2026. Its product based on phosphorus-solubilising bacteria is in field trials, and a liquid inoculant and a mycorrhizal product are in development, all based on locally isolated strains.
02Quality control

Every certificate of analysis names the method behind each result

Every production batch is tested and released against a certificate of analysis from our own laboratory. The format is consistent across the catalogue: physical status, moisture, particle size and pH, followed by the chemical assays, each carrying its specification, its result and the method used to obtain it.

RuleWhat it means
No lot ships without a COAEvery batch is released against the frozen specification, and every lot ships with the certificates of analysis (COA) of its batches.
Every assay names its methodThis allows a third-party result to be compared like for like.
A specification change is documentedThe partner is told before the change takes effect, so nothing is substituted without notice.

03The research farms

Every formulation is tested on calcareous field soil, not only on the bench

Our first agricultural experimental station was established in 2009, and we run our own research farms, staffed by agricultural scientists, for product trials. Farm trials show where a formulation fails before a client relies on it for a season.

04Licences and certification

PSQCA licences and ISO 9001 certification

We hold 35 PSQCA (Pakistan Standards and Quality Control Authority) licences. Of these licences, 23 are for our own brands. The other 12 licences are for registered formulations that a partner company can sell under its own brand. The Regulatory and registration services page lists the licence number of each of our brands that held a licence in July 2026.

ISO 9001 quality management certification is in progress with System Certification Centre (SCC) and is expected during 2027. We will not claim it until the certificate is issued.

The licence count is as of November 2026.
