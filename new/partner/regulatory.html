Fertiliser registration with PSQCA: our licences and our service for partners

A fertiliser that is not registered cannot be sold. If registration is late and a product misses its selling season, the product has to wait for the next season. VAN registers its own brands with PSQCA (Pakistan Standards and Quality Control Authority) itself. 20 of our 24 brands hold a live PSQCA licence. PSQCA has also approved a licence for Nitro Sulfur, our 24th brand. 12 more products are already licensed and ready for a partner to launch within 60 days. We also offer this registration work to partners as a separate service.

02How registration works

Registration takes 4 steps, and we control the first 2

StepWhat happens
Standard selectionWe establish which Pakistan Standard (PS) the product falls under and whether the intended analysis fits within it. A mistake at this step is the most expensive one in the process.
TestingAnalysis against the standard, with our own laboratory data and certificates supporting the file.
Dossier and filingPreparation and filing with PSQCA, including the manufacturing evidence that an importer or a trader cannot supply.
LicencePSQCA issues the manufacturing licence, and the product can then be sold legally anywhere in Pakistan.

We complete the first 2 steps within 3 weeks. The last 2 depend on the regulator and typically take 1 to 3 months.

Every VAN product has a complete technical file, and a partner product is given the same file from the start: a 1-page technical data sheet, a 16-section GHS (Globally Harmonized System) safety data sheet with a hazard classification for that product rather than a general template, and a certificate of analysis for each production batch from our analytical laboratory, giving the specification, result and method for every assay.
