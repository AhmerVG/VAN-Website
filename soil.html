Reading it · Phosphorus

97.9% of samples are below 15 ppm, the adequate level.

The colour
Rust at 3 ppm and below, gold at 7.5, green from 12; Punjab calls below 7 low and 15 adequate. Half of Punjab’s samples read below 6.4 ppm, the median.
Why it matters for the crop
Phosphorus carries energy inside the plant and builds the root. It decides how fast a seedling puts down roots, how many tillers wheat makes, and how well the crop sets and fills. Most of the damage is done in the first weeks and cannot be undone later.
What Punjab’s ground does to it
Calcareous soil binds phosphate with free calcium and takes most of it back before the root arrives.

Sulfur has no map, because it was never measured. See the analyte list below.

Punjab tested 770,160 soil samples for pH, organic matter, phosphorus, potash, calcium carbonate and 5 micronutrients. Read together, they describe 1 soil: alkaline, calcareous, very low in organic matter, short of phosphorus, potash, zinc and boron in most of them, and never tested for sulfur.

What this ground costs the country

On this soil, adding more product does not solve the problem.

Punjab's ground is alkaline and calcareous. 96.1% of samples above pH 7.5, mean carbonate 6.53%. Across Pakistan, nutrient applied per acre has risen by 68% since 2000 while wheat yield rose by 31%. Roughly 1 kg of applied nitrogen in 4 reaches the crop.

Sources · nutrient per acre of arable land 1999-2000 to 2023-24: World Bank; wheat yield: Pakistan Economic Survey; nitrogen reaching the crop: Lassaletta et al. (2014), Environmental Research Letters 9:105011.

What changes the outcome is the form the nutrient arrives in and where it is put, not the tonnage.

Method & caveats

36 district workbooks from the Punjab soil testing programme, each row 1 georeferenced sample carrying district, tehsil, markaz, union council and mauza identifiers alongside the laboratory determinations. Every row was read; no sub-sampling. Records without a valid pH were excluded; 770,160 remained. Province figures are weighted by district sample count.

Age

The sampling window is roughly 2016–2018. These figures describe a structural baseline, not present-day status.

Observational

Survey data with no controlled treatments. It establishes conditions with very high confidence. It cannot establish mechanism; for that, controlled and peer-reviewed work is the appropriate evidence.

Thresholds

Deficiency shares use Punjab’s working thresholds: organic matter below 0.86%, available phosphorus below 7 ppm (adequate at 15), available potash below 180 ppm, zinc below 1.0 ppm, boron below 0.5 ppm.

Corrections

1 column in the Sheikhupura workbook was repeated by mistake; it was corrected before the figures were worked out. Jhelum returns no valid zinc data. Physically impossible values were bounded before averaging.

Bounds. Physically impossible values were bounded before averaging: pH 0–14, OM 0–10%, P 0–200 ppm, CaCO3 0–100%, K 0–3000 ppm, Zn 0–50 ppm, B 0–20 ppm, EC 0–100 dS/m.
